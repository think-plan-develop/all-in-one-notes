🧪 Evaluation & Completion Checks

Use a small, manually verified reference set. Create the development examples early, then keep the fourth synthetic case separate until Day 29.

What to measureHow to check it
Field extractionCorrect extracted values divided by labeled fields; report missing/ambiguous fields separately.
Mismatch detectionReport true findings, false alarms and missed known mismatches; compute precision and recall from those counts.
RetrievalCheck whether the relevant eligible source passage appears in the returned top-k results.
AnswersJudge correctness and whether each substantive claim is supported by the cited passage.
AbstentionCheck whether the app appropriately declines to answer questions without sufficient evidence.
CitationsVerify source ownership, document version, page and actual support for the answer.
AuthorizationTest other-case IDs, private notes, source downloads, tools and exports.
OperationsRecord observed duration, failed calls, retry behavior, tokens and estimated cost.
Reporting ruleReport results on the actual small sample with denominators and examples. Development scores are for tuning. Held-out results must remain separate; if you tune on a held-out failure, label the rerun accordingly.
