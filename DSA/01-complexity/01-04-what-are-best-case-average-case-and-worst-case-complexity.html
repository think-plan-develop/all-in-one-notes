01.04 - What are best-case, average-case and worst-case complexity?

  • Best case: the least work for any input of size n.
  • Average case: the expected work under a stated distribution of inputs.
  • Worst case: the greatest work for any input of size n.
Linear search: if the target is first, the best case is O(1). If its position is uniformly distributed, average work is O(n). If it is last or absent, worst-case work is O(n).

Big-O, Big-Omega, and Big-Theta describe upper, lower, and tight asymptotic bounds; they do not inherently mean worst, best, or average case. Specify both the case being analyzed and the bound being used.

Key idea: case analysis is about which inputs are considered; asymptotic notation is about bounding the resulting growth.
